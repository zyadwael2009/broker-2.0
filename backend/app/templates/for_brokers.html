How it works

Three steps. About forty-eight hours.

  1. 01

    Submit your GOEIC registration

    Sign up in the app, enter your GOEIC number, and upload a photo or scan of your registration document. That’s the whole form.

  2. 02

Common questions

Straight answers.

How much does verification cost?

Free during our launch. We’ll be transparent well in advance if that changes.

What documents do I need?

Your GOEIC registration certificate. A clear photo or scan is fine. That’s it. We never ask for national-ID card or bank details.

What if my GOEIC is expired or being renewed?

We’ll tell you exactly what’s missing. If it’s a renewal in progress you can resubmit as soon as it’s reissued.

Can I use Wasit while I’m still pending review?

You can sign in, but posting listings and sending messages are gated on verification. This is the whole point — buyers only ever see verified brokers.

What happens if I get rejected?

You get a specific reason and can resubmit with corrected documents. No cooldown, no penalty.

Do buyers see my phone number?

Only inside the app after they open a thread with you. Your public /b/ URL never shows your phone or email — everything goes through in-app messaging.
