1. Acceptance

By creating a Wasit account or using wasit.app, you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the service.

2. Who can use Wasit

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must use your real name and a phone number you actually control. Fake accounts are removed on discovery.
  • Broker accounts require a valid GOEIC registration document that passes our review.

3. What Wasit does — and what it doesn’t

Wasit helps you verify: we check that brokers are registered with GOEIC and give buyers a place to see that verification alongside a broker’s active listings and reviews. That’s the whole product.

Wasit is not a lawyer, a notary, an escrow agent, or a brokerage. A property listing being on Wasit does not mean we’ve inspected the property, verified the title independently at the Real Estate Registry, or confirmed the seller’s intent. Always do those checks in person, with a lawyer, and at the Registry before signing any contract.

4. Broker responsibilities

  • Your listings must be for real, currently-available properties you have permission to represent.
  • Photos must be your own (or licensed) and depict the actual unit being listed.
  • Prices must be genuine offers, not lead-generation lures.
  • Document uploads (title deed, no-liens certificate, tax clearance) must be authentic. Uploading forged documents leads to immediate account removal and may be reported.

5. Buyer responsibilities

  • Communicate with brokers respectfully.
  • Rate honestly, and only after a real interaction.
  • Report abuse (fraud, spam, inappropriate messages) via the report button on any listing or broker profile.

6. Prohibited content

You may not post or send:

  • Anything illegal in Egypt.
  • Discriminatory listings (rentals must comply with fair-housing norms).
  • Duplicated listings with someone else’s photos.
  • Off-platform contact solicitations before a genuine listing conversation.
