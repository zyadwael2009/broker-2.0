### Full description (EN)

```
Wasit is the credential platform for Egyptian real-estate brokers — and
the safest way for buyers to find one they can trust.

**Every broker on Wasit is verified.**
Before a broker can post a single listing, we review their GOEIC
(General Organization for Export and Import Control) registration
document by hand. Buyers see a big Verified badge, the GOEIC number,
and the verification date on every profile.

**Every listing carries its papers.**
For each property, brokers upload the title deed, no-liens certificate,
and tax clearance — or clearly mark them as self-reported. Buyers know
exactly what's checked before they call.

**Ratings that mean something.**
Only buyers who've actually messaged a broker can rate them. No
drive-by stars, no fake reviews.

**In-app messaging.**
Every conversation lives alongside the listing and its documents.
Nothing gets lost in WhatsApp screenshots.

**Wasit assists with verification — it does not replace a lawyer or
the notary office.** Always confirm ownership at the Real Estate
Registry before signing.

Built for Egypt: bilingual English + Arabic, RTL-first, phone-based
sign-in, and works on any Android phone.
```
